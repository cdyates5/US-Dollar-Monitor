US-minus-Global Manufacturing Momentum & the US Dollar

Relative manufacturing-survey momentum between the US and its major trading partners tends to lead the US dollar. When US factories out-accelerate China, the euro area and Japan, the dollar has historically firmed several months later. The differential is advanced months to line it up against DXY year-on-year change — a reconstruction of the BCA Research / Clocktower chart family.
