Construction. Twin deficit = federal budget balance (BEA NIPA: current receipts − current expenditures, SAAR, ÷ GDP) + current-account balance (BEA ITA IEABC from 1999, spliced to legacy BOP BOPBCA before 1999; annualised ÷ GDP). Series is a 4-quarter trailing mean, then shifted +7 quarters (s.index = s.index + 7) so it plots as a lead. DXY is the ICE U.S. Dollar Index (6-currency), month-end. Recessions are NBER (FRED USREC).
Honest caveats. Stouff/Datastream do not publish their exact fiscal series; a 4Q-average NIPA definition puts the 2020–21 trough near −21% vs the source’s ~−22% (raw single-quarter NIPA net saving spikes deeper, to ~−30%). The 7Q lead is taken as-labelled from the source, not re-optimised — on raw levels the lead/DXY co-movement is modest (secular trend + the COVID outlier); it is clearest across medium-term cycles and turning points. The right-hand shaded band is a pure time shift of realised data, not a model forecast of the dollar.
