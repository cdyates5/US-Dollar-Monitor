FX Hedging Pressure

The Brigden/Nordvig channel, institutionalised by the BIS after April 2025: foreigners hold $30T+ of US assets, roughly half of Europe's holdings unhedged, and their hedge ratios rise precisely when the dollar stops being a natural hedge — when equities and the dollar start falling together. Rising hedge ratios mean forward dollar selling. This gauge tracks the trigger: the rolling correlation between S&P 500 and DXY daily returns.

The natural-hedge gauge
