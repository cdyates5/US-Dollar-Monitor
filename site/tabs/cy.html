Treasury Premium — a reconstruction stress-test

The Jiang–Krishnamurthy–Lustig convenience-yield channel says foreign demand for the safety of Treasuries drives the dollar. This dashboard attempts to reconstruct their Treasury basis from public data — twice — and documents plainly that it cannot be done without FX forward points. What survives is a crisis flight-to-bills gauge and a reference to the published facts.

Two proxies vs the dollar

Both proxies approximate the FX hedge with the 3-month interbank differential (CIP with money rates). Proxy A hedges 1-year government yields; proxy B is matched-tenor — the relative 3-month bill–interbank spread. Positive = Treasuries relatively special. Both spike in genuine dollar-funding crises (labelled) and both are otherwise dominated by components that are not convenience. DXY YoY on the right axis. Gray bands are US recessions.

The failed replication

JKL's core fact: convenience-yield innovations coincide with dollar appreciation — their basis explains up to 41% of quarterly dollar variation. On the public-data proxies, the same test yields approximately nothing, with the wrong sign.
