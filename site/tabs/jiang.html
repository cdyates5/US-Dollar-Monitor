US Fiscal Cycle & the Dollar

Jiang (JME 2021): the US government surplus-to-debt ratio predicts next-year dollar excess returns — a fiscal-condition risk premium built on Treasuries' reserve-asset role. We reconstruct the predictor from public data, replicate the direction inside his 1988–2017 window at a fraction of the published strength, and show the relationship has been dead-to-inverted since.

The ratio (12-month federal budget balance over debt outstanding, orange, left) against the realised next-12-month dollar excess return — spot plus the US-minus-foreign short-rate carry — stamped at the period it covers (ink, right). The dashed line maps the ratio through the full-sample fit, also advanced 12 months. Gray bands are US recessions.
